Activate Modbus TCP Communication
Anker SOLIX V1 Smart EV Charger supports the Modbus TCP communication protocol 
for integration with external monitoring or control systems through Wi-Fi or Ethernet. 
This enables energy management systems or supervisory controllers to start or stop 
charging, monitor charger status, collect operational data, and manage charging 
operations. 
To Activate Modbus TCP
1. Open the Anker app, select V1 Smart EV Charger, and tap the Settings icon on the 
device details page.
2. Select Integrations from the menu.
3. Toggle the switch to enable the Modbus TCP function.
